About the Club

The Tahoma Rocketry Club is a place for students to learn hands-on engineering skills. We don't just build kits; we design custom rockets, simulate flights, and launch them to thousands of feet.

📐

Design

Using OpenRocket and CAD software, we engineer stable and efficient rockets from scratch.

🛠️

Build

We use advanced materials like fiberglass and carbon fiber, along with 3D printing, to bring designs to life.

🚀

Launch

We attend regional launches and national competitions, certifying members for high-power rocketry.
